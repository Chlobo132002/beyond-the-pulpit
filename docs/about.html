About the Initiative

About the Resilience in Leadership Initiative

The Resilience in Leadership Initiative (RELI) at Chicago Theological Seminary (CTS) supports clergy, ministers, lay leaders, and seminary students who serve historically marginalized and disenfranchised communities.

Through mentorship, leadership development, peer relationships, and wellness-focused programming, RELI seeks to strengthen vocational resilience and promote sustainable ministry.

The initiative recognizes that faith leaders serve as spiritual guides, community advocates, and sources of emotional support. Sustaining these responsibilities requires attention not only to professional development, but also to personal health and well-being.

The Clergy Wellness Pilot

Supported through CTS’s Thriving in Ministry programming, the RELI Clergy Wellness Pilot brought together expertise in pastoral leadership, spiritual formation, physical wellness, public health, and technology.

The pilot was designed to explore a more holistic approach to supporting clergy wellness, recognizing the connections among physical health, emotional resilience, spiritual renewal, and leadership sustainability.

This website examines that work through a public health and data visualization lens.

Meet the Team

The initiative benefited from a multidisciplinary team whose expertise spans faith leadership, coaching, wellness, technology, and public health evaluation.

Rev. Dr. Brian E. Smith, DMin, MHSA, MDiv, is a faith leader, community partnership strategist, and the Program Convenor and Director of the RELI Clergy Wellness Initiative.

Through his work at Chicago Theological Seminary, Dr. Smith has supported initiatives connecting theological education, community engagement, and leadership development. His professional experience includes building partnerships with faith-based organizations, developing community-centered programs, and supporting clergy serving historically underserved communities.

Dr. Smith also serves as a pastor and has contributed to projects involving Black faith leadership, community development, and the preservation of religious and civil rights history.

His leadership in the RELI Clergy Wellness Initiative reflects a commitment to ensuring that clergy receive the resources, encouragement, and institutional support necessary to sustain their ministries.

Rev. Dr. James D. Foster, PhD, MDiv, is a theologian, pastor, author, and leadership coach whose professional work focuses on strengthening faith-based leadership and organizational effectiveness.

His experience includes pastoral ministry, theological education, leadership development, and coaching for mission-driven organizations.

As the initiative’s Certified Leadership Coach and Spiritual Advisor, Dr. Foster contributes expertise in spiritual formation, leadership resilience, personal development, and the challenges associated with sustaining effective ministry.

His perspective reinforces the importance of connecting professional leadership growth with personal reflection, spiritual well-being, and long-term vocational purpose.

Larry Love, MS, AT, ATC, CSC, is a wellness and performance professional whose work emphasizes physical health, movement, personal wellness, and sustainable lifestyle practices.

Through Total Body Wellness & Performance, Love has provided wellness education tailored to individuals whose professional responsibilities can make consistent self-care challenging.

His work with faith leaders emphasizes the importance of attending to physical health while managing the demands of caregiving and ministry.

As Wellness and Performance Coach, Love brings an applied health and wellness perspective to the RELI team, helping connect the initiative’s broader mission of clergy resilience with practical approaches to physical well-being.

Christian Horn, BBA, is a healthcare technology entrepreneur whose professional interests include digital innovation, data-informed decision-making, and the application of technology to health and wellness.

Identified in the RELI evaluation report as an investor and founder of DYMND.AI, Horn brings a technology and business perspective to the multidisciplinary initiative.

His inclusion on the team reflects the potential role of digital tools, data systems, and technology-enabled monitoring in supporting wellness programming and program evaluation.

Within the broader context of RELI, this expertise complements the pastoral, coaching, and public health perspectives represented by other team members.

Chloe A. Smith, BSPH, is a public health professional and Master of Public Health candidate at Morehouse School of Medicine, with experience in program coordination, community engagement, evaluation, and data visualization.

Her work spans public health research, organizational programming, stakeholder engagement, and the translation of health information into accessible communication products.

Through her work with Chicago Theological Seminary, Smith has supported clergy wellness programming through coordination, evaluation-related activities, and the integration of public health perspectives into faith-based initiatives.

Her contributions to Beyond the Pulpit reflect an interest in connecting public health practice with faith institutions, using data storytelling to communicate program experiences and inform future opportunities for health promotion.

A Multidisciplinary Approach to Clergy Wellness

The RELI team brings together five complementary areas of expertise:

  • Faith and community leadership: Connecting clergy wellness to the broader mission of sustainable ministry.
  • Spiritual formation and leadership coaching: Supporting reflection, personal development, and vocational resilience.
  • Physical wellness and performance: Emphasizing movement, health education, and sustainable self-care.
  • Healthcare technology and data: Exploring opportunities to support wellness monitoring and information management.
  • Public health and program evaluation: Translating program information into evidence that can inform communication and future planning.

Together, these perspectives illustrate why clergy wellness extends beyond any single discipline.

About This Website

Beyond the Pulpit is an MPH 718 data visualization project developed by Chloe A. Smith.

The website draws upon existing RELI program documentation, evaluation materials, and publicly available information to communicate the initiative’s purpose and public health relevance.
